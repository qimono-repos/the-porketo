# Governance Refinement · 2026-09-30

## Purpose

Record the governance refinements established during the September 30, 2026 project reasoning session.

The journal is the AI's durable memory of project reasoning. It is not a waiver form, approval checklist, or replacement for source records.

## Journal Role

The journal should normally rewrite conversation material into concise, durable reasoning.

When the operator's original wording contains an important insight, definition, principle, conceptual distinction, or breakthrough, the AI may preserve that wording alongside the concise reasoning.

This is the adopted hybrid approach:

- concise AI-written reasoning by default;
- original wording preserved selectively when the wording itself carries durable conceptual value.

The journal therefore preserves reasoning without becoming a transcript graveyard.

## Precedent Handling

The AI identifies and proposes the **single best precedent** for a new case.

The operator is not required to formally cite or accept a precedent during the conversation. The AI records the relevant precedent and reasoning in the journal.

The operator may intervene to overrule or distinguish the proposed precedent.

## Exceptions and Dispositions

When an exception or deviation from an established rule is recorded, the journal includes a disposition.

The disposition records the status of the case, such as:

- Accepted
- Rejected
- Unresolved

An exception record should preserve the reasoning behind the departure from the normal rule.

## Deviation

A deviation is a material difference between prescribed or planned behavior and observed or executed behavior.

Examples include differences in:

- quantity;
- percentage;
- timing;
- sequence;
- frequency;
- scope; or
- condition.

A deviation is not automatically an error. The journal records the deviation, its rationale, action taken, outcome, and disposition.

## Precedent and Evolution

Precedents do not expire merely because time passes.

A precedent remains available as historical project knowledge unless it is explicitly overruled. Its applicability to a later case must be evaluated against the circumstances of that case.

When a new case is sufficiently analogous, the AI should identify the single best precedent and incorporate its reasoning. A later case may distinguish or overrule the precedent when the circumstances or rationale justify doing so.

An overrule must preserve the historical precedent and record the rationale for the change.

## First Case and Later Precedent

The first occurrence of a new type of case receives a full journal treatment, including rationale and outcome.

Later sufficiently similar cases can rely on that earlier case as precedent rather than recreating the entire reasoning from scratch.

This creates a growing body of project experience and jurisprudence while preserving the original history.

## Core Principle

The project rules evolve visibly.

When a rule changes, the previous rule or reasoning is not silently rewritten. The historical version remains available and the new reasoning records why it supersedes or overrules what came before.

## Source Record Boundary

The journal does not replace execution or bookkeeping source records.

The established operating path remains:

real broker/exchange fill → source trade log → derived positions → strategy cockpit → dashboard.

Execution history remains the source truth; the journal is the reasoning and experience layer above it.
