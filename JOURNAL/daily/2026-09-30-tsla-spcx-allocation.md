## Durable Reasoning

The operator intentionally departed from the previously discussed equal $10/$10 split.

The reason is not a declaration that Tesla is the better long-term company or investment. The allocation is a deliberate tactical tilt toward the leg judged to have greater near-term instability and event sensitivity, while retaining SpaceX as a distinct diversification leg.

The key conceptual distinction is:

> business diversification and investment-risk diversification are related but not identical.

TSLA and SPCX represent materially different operating businesses, but both remain high-growth technology exposures and share meaningful Musk ecosystem and market-sentiment risk.

## Precedent

Relevant precedent: the project's diversification principle, **“Diversify economically, but stay inside our intellectual horizon.”**

The prior TSLA/SPCX analysis established that an equal split would provide the cleanest test of the diversification thesis. This case deliberately distinguishes that precedent because the operator wants a larger TSLA exposure for the immediate catalyst window.

## Deviation / Disposition

**Deviation:** prior equal-split suggestion of $10/$10 changed to $15 TSLA / $5 SPCX.

**Disposition:** Accepted.

**Rationale:** increase exposure to the more event-sensitive leg while preserving a non-zero SpaceX diversification position.
