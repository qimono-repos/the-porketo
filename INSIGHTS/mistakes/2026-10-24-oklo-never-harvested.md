## Why it never harvested

1. **We bought into a long downtrend, not a dip.** It was −62% from the peak and still falling. "Biggest dip" (40% of the score) looked attractive, but momentum (5%) was negative, and nothing in the score said "the fall hasn't stopped".
2. **Dilution, the day after we bought.** A pre-revenue company burning cash pays its bills by selling new shares. Each share sold puts a lid on rallies. That is exactly what an anchor-based harvest needs, and it didn't come.
3. **No catalyst in our time frame.** Revenue is 2027 to 2028 at the earliest. Without a near catalyst, the price only moves with the AI/growth mood, and that mood turned down in October.
4. **One high lot, never laddered.** No lower lot was ever added, so there was nothing that a small bounce could lift above its anchor.
5. **Wrapper.** The Binance lot is a plain Binance Stock (0.17 fee, ~5.17 minimum), which makes micro-harvests impractical even after a bounce.

## Lessons (proposed rules, pending Albert)

- **Dilution check before buying pre-revenue companies:** look for an open ATM / shelf offering or a cash runway under 2 years. If there is one, the asset is capped at tier C, or we wait for the offering to be absorbed.
- **A dip needs a floor:** if the 2-month trend is still making lower lows, it counts as a falling knife, not a dip. Wait for one higher low before tier A or B.
- **Catalyst inside the horizon:** for our 6-hour / weekly harvest cadence, prefer assets with a catalyst within ~3 months.
- **Ladder or leave:** a single high lot that is −20% with no plan to add lower is a candidate to stop feeding.
- **Same risk elsewhere:** pre-revenue quantum names (XNDU, PSQL, INFQ, QBTS, IONQ) can dilute the same way. Keep the quantum sleeve (Albert's decision 2026-10-24), but apply the dilution check to each new buy.
