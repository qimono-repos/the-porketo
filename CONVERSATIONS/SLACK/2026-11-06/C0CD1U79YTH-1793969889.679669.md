this message is on behalf of @Claude ai

Re topic of the day (what to migrate first, finance + DDD): I agree with ChatGPT that the event ledger comes first, but our own design doc already settled two things its list leaves out. See `no-sql/DDD.md` (<https://github.com/qimono-repos/the-porketo/blob/trunk/no-sql/DDD.md|github.com/qimono-repos/the-porketo/blob/…/DDD.md>).

1. *Ledger events (migrate first).* These are the only facts. Everything else can be rebuilt from them. The sources are the book's *Trades* tab (header A1:Q1, rows 2–45, 44 events) and the *Crypto Trades* tab (A1:W1, ~113 events). The tabs use different schemas: stocks have "Converted Amount ($)" + "Cash Flow ($)", and crypto has "Quote Asset", "Fee Asset/Qty" and separate USDC/USDT wallet flows. So we need one versioned event contract with `book: stocks|crypto`, and not two collections.
2. *Lots (projection, not migrated).* DDD.md makes the Lot the aggregate root. Its natural key is ticker + entry price + opened date, and its material anchor is immutable. ChatGPT's list has no Lot. Without it, the harvest signal can't be computed from the DB, and that signal is the reason we're moving off the sheet. Rebuild lots from events. Don't copy them from Positions / Crypto Positions.
3. *Capital flows as first-class events.* FUND (CRF-_), FX (CFX-_) and withdrawals, like the ~91 USDT that left on 2026-10-07 in `migration.md`. DDD.md §9 flags that the stock tab has no row type for these yet.
4. *Instruments and marks next,* as ChatGPT said. IONQ, IONQUSDT perp and the *B wrappers stay separate instruments. Price Log is just observations.
5. *Forecasts are not ledger.* In Crypto Trades, columns R–W ("Forecast Value…" through "SPE…") are decision/forecast data. Split them into their own collection. Slots!F is derived from events, so don't import it as truth.
Two blockers I found in the book before any idempotent import:
• *Duplicate Event ID.* In Crypto Trades, A15 and A52 are both `CRF-0002`. A15 is the 2026-09-13 BNB→3.58 USDC fund and A52 is the 2026-10-06 36.79 USDC fund. With `_id = eventId`, the second one is rejected. One of them needs a new ID.
• *Blank rows and a mixed time format.* Crypto Trades A102:A104 are empty rows. Column B mixes "ART" and "Halifax" text (e.g. B52), so UTC conversion needs a rule per row.
Also, `no-sql/stock-trades.json` is a snapshot from 2026-09-30 with 24 events. The Trades tab now has 44, so that file is stale.

Suggested next step: I draft `no-sql/ledger-event-contract.md` (field-by-field mapping of both tabs → one schema) once you say go. Nothing gets booked in Mongo without your explicit OK.
