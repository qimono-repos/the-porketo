# Projection by Event 98 — SECOND DRAFT

**Status:** Second rough draft, criteria-focused  
**Horizon:** Event 98 / Checkpoint 98, Friday 30 October 2026, 22:00 Halifax time  
**Squad:** 35 players across crypto and tokenised stocks  
**Purpose:** Define the capital-allocation criteria and intended outcome, not certify exact execution totals.

## Objective

By Event 98, move the 35-player squad toward a more equitable distribution of invested capital, with an approximate planning objective of **$10 invested per player wherever the available deployment budget permits**.

The approximately $10 figure is a rough target, not a promise that every player will finish at the same amount. The governing principle is to improve equity by prioritising the least-funded players.

## Allocation criterion: bottom-up capital leveling

1. Treat crypto assets and tokenised stocks as one unified squad. Each underlying asset is one player, even if positions or lots originate from different engines or venues.
2. Compare players by **invested capital**, not current market value.
3. Allocate available deployment capital to the player or players with the lowest invested capital first.
4. Raise the bottom of the squad progressively toward the emerging waterline. Do not give additional capital to players already above that waterline while lower-funded players remain below it.
5. Continue this bottom-up process until the deployment budget is exhausted or the intended leveling objective has been reached as far as the budget and execution constraints permit.

This is not an equal split of the budget across 35 players. It is a leveling rule: the lowest-funded players receive priority, while better-funded players can remain above the waterline without being reduced.

## Working assumptions for this rough projection

- Squad size: **35 players**.
- Remaining deployment budget: **$65.25 USDC**, based on 87 checkpoints at $0.75 each.
- Starting invested capital: approximately **$430**.
- Projected invested capital after deployment: approximately **$495**, using rounded starting figures.
- The first-draft calculation suggested a theoretical waterline in the neighborhood of **$10 to $11**, with approximately 15 of the 35 players receiving top-ups. These are planning estimates, not exact or guaranteed endpoints.
- For this thought experiment, the additional Binance lots are accepted as inputs without reconciling individual movements. This does not convert them into verified execution records for accounting or operational purposes.

## Practical execution constraints

Deployment slices of $0.75 and venue-specific minimum order sizes may prevent the actual ending anchors from matching the theoretical waterline exactly. Execution order is secondary to the allocation rule: the intended result is the same bottom-up leveling, subject to what can actually be deployed.

Harvests do not increase a player's invested-capital anchor under this concept. Market value, unrealised gains and losses, and risk equivalence are outside the scope of this projection.

## What success means at Event 98

Success does **not** mean every player has exactly $10 invested. It means the available deployment budget has been used according to the bottom-up rule, giving the least-funded players priority and bringing the squad toward the approximate $10-per-player objective as far as the budget and practical constraints allow.

## Interpretation and verification boundary

This is a rough planning model for refining the criteria. It is not an execution ledger, a reconciled account of fills, or a guarantee of the final invested-capital distribution. Broker and exchange receipts remain execution truth; operational records must distinguish verified executions from workbook values, estimates, and assumptions.
