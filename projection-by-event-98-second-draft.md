## Objective and intended outcome

By Event 98, the intention is for **all 35 players to have at least approximately $10 in invested capital each**. The word *approximately* is deliberate: $10 is a rough planning floor, not a demand for an exact identical balance.

For this planning exercise, **assume the funds required to pursue this objective will be available**. Do not repeatedly challenge the objective on the basis that the currently stated working budget may be insufficient. The purpose of this draft is to make the allocation criteria and intended outcome clear. Budget feasibility is an assumption of this thought experiment, not a question to reopen at every step.

The deployment is planned as a cadence of movements through Event 98. The intention is to continue applying the allocation rule through the final movement, so that the final $0.75 slice is assigned to a player who still needs capital to reach the approximate $10 floor. This describes the intended plan; it is not a claim that future executions have already occurred.

## Allocation criterion: bottom-up capital leveling

1. Treat crypto assets and tokenised stocks as one unified squad of **35 players**. Each underlying asset is one player, even if positions or lots originate from different engines or venues.
2. Compare players by **invested capital**, not current market value.
3. Allocate deployment capital to the player or players with the lowest invested capital first.
4. Progressively raise the least-funded players toward the approximate $10 floor. Players already above the current leveling point receive no additional capital while another player remains below the objective and requires funding.
5. Continue this bottom-up process through the planned cadence to Event 98, assigning the final planned $0.75 movement to a player who still needs capital, with the intention that all 35 players finish at approximately $10 or more invested.

This is **not** an equal split of the budget across 35 players. It is a leveling rule: the least-funded players receive priority, while better-funded players can remain above the floor without being reduced.

## What success means at Event 98

Success means **aiming for all 35 players to finish with at least approximately $10 in invested capital each**, using the bottom-up allocation rule and the planned deployment cadence. The $10 figure is a rough floor, not an average across the team and not a requirement that every player finish at exactly the same amount.

The core idea is equitable invested-capital leveling: prioritize the least-funded players until each has reached the approximate floor, while leaving already better-funded players above it.
