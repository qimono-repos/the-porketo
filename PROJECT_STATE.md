# Project State

## Project

A personal trading, investment, and financial automation laboratory.

The project has two purposes that are treated as one unified system:

- Serve as a primary personal income resource through trading and investment activity.
- Serve as a laboratory for researching and developing automation of financial transactions, with the goal of executing transactions faster than a human can.

## Role

The project owner is explicitly both:

- **Operator**: the person making, supervising, and evaluating financial decisions and transactions.
- **Test subject**: the person whose finances, decisions, results, and learning process provide real-world evidence for the project's hypotheses.

## Intermediate Goal

Publish the project's progress, experiments, methodology, and results so that the development of the system can eventually be communicated publicly, including through professional social networks such as LinkedIn and X.

## Long-Term Direction

Develop a documented, evidence-based system for personal finance, investment, trading, and transaction automation, while preserving the ability to examine whether the underlying theses hold up through real-world results.

## Current Phase

The project is in the **middle of an ongoing experiment**.

The trading and investment activity already exists. The current work is to develop, document, measure, and progressively automate the system around that activity.

The experiment uses the project owner as both operator and test subject, with real financial activity providing evidence for the project's theses.

A core part of the experiment is **regular public reporting of progress and results**, with a minimum cadence of monthly reports published through platforms such as LinkedIn and X.

The repository and knowledge system are being developed in parallel with the experiment so that decisions, hypotheses, experiments, evidence, results, and lessons learned remain documented and reproducible.

## Current Focus

Establish a sustainable daily harvesting rhythm of **0.75 SDC per day on average** during the next 30 days, beginning **October 1, 2026**.

The objective is not to require 0.75 SDC on every individual day from the beginning. The experiment is expected to start from the project's current state, with smaller daily harvests during the initial days, and progressively develop toward the target rhythm.

The primary measurement for this phase is therefore the **average daily harvest over the full 30-day period**, with daily harvesting activity recorded throughout the experiment.

The immediate goal is to establish and demonstrate a repeatable harvesting capability under real operating conditions.

## Active Theses

### Core Hypothesis

With sufficient investment in time, knowledge, and work with AI companions, a system can be developed that becomes self-sustainable by generating profit through the repeated harvesting of marginal gains from positions in stocks and cryptocurrencies.

The system's intended growth target is approximately **5% of the original investment**, with AI assistance becoming an increasingly important component of the operating system.

### AI-Assisted Knowledge and Selection

AI companions are part of the project's **knowledge-generation and investment-selection process**, not only portfolio monitoring.

The operating loop is:

**AI-assisted research → knowledge accumulation → candidate identification → investment or exclusion decision → real financial move when applicable → continuous AI-assisted monitoring → harvest opportunity → harvest move → evidence → improved knowledge.**

The system can therefore produce both positive selections and explicit decisions not to invest. Recent examples include research leading to investments such as IonQ, D-Wave, BlackRock, and Smooth Love Potion, as well as research contributing to a decision not to pursue AES as a diversification candidate because of its announced take-private transaction.

### Portfolio Orientation

The stock portfolio is primarily oriented toward:

1. Quantum computing and quantum technology companies.
2. AI-related companies.
3. Diversification across relevant opportunities.

The cryptocurrency portfolio is primarily oriented toward:

1. Layer 1 cryptocurrencies.
2. Gaming cryptocurrencies.
3. Infrastructure cryptocurrencies.
4. Specific attention to Robinhood and Mint coins as infrastructure.

The underlying thesis is that sufficient **diversity and operational volume** can create enough opportunities to repeatedly harvest marginal gains while developing toward a self-sustainable system.

## Active Research

### Cryptocurrency Research

The primary cryptocurrency research priority is **gaming cryptocurrencies**.

The current research includes the mechanics and ecosystem structures of projects such as:

- Smooth Love Potion (SLP)
- Axie Infinity
- Ronin
- Solana

The broader objective is to identify and understand additional **full-stack gaming ecosystems** so that the portfolio can be diversified across multiple independent ecosystems rather than relying on a small number of projects.

**Meme coins are a secondary research area**, to be addressed after the gaming crypto research has been sufficiently developed.

### Stock Diversification Research

Continue refining diversification of stock assets by identifying companies whose performance is not strongly correlated with the growth or decline of artificial intelligence.

The objective is to include businesses that can provide diversification when AI-related assets move in either direction, rather than concentrating the portfolio's exposure around a single dominant theme.

## Recent Decisions
- **Crisis Harvest mechanics formalized:** when the final-harvest-day condition activates and normal harvesting has not occurred by market close, select the open strategy lot with the smallest absolute USD/USDC-equivalent gap to its harvest threshold among lots below threshold. Liberate 90% of that lot's material anchor, leave 10% invested, preserve lot identity and anchor, and calculate the native Binance FROM quantity from the notification snapshot. If the available position at execution is smaller than the specified quantity, sell the entire remaining position rather than canceling the crisis movement.

- The canonical trading workflow is already established in the trading book and should be evolved rather than redesigned from scratch.
- AI-assisted research is a core part of knowledge generation and asset selection, including decisions not to invest.
- The complete historical archaeology of prior ChatGPT conversations is not required before continuing the experiment.
- **From this point forward, when a conversation produces an investment or exclusion decision, a short decision record will be captured in the repository.**
- Decision records should preserve, at minimum: **asset, decision, why, key knowledge gained, and date**.
